CURVED RESOURCE HUD 0.6.9 UPPER ICON ALIGNMENT TEST CHECKLIST

1. In Bethesda.net, set required dependencies to:
   - LibAddonMenu-2.0
   - LibVotans (this is LibHarvensAddonSettings inside ESO)
   The manifest keeps both optional so a missing menu library cannot block the HUD.

2. Install/update, enable CurvedHUD, then reload the UI or relog.

3. Confirm chat prints:
   [CurvedHUD] Loaded 0.6.9-test; HUD, shield, mount stamina, and trackers created

4. Type /curvedhud preview. Expected:
   - red health arc on the left
   - green stamina inner-right (default)
   - blue magicka outer-right
   - raw values over percentages, with Stamina and Magicka in separate rows
   - gray shield overlay on Health
   - green Major Resolve and orange Balance-family test trackers beside Health

5. Open and close inventory/settings. CurvedHUD must disappear with the HUD scene and
   return after the menu closes. Enable Preview Mode from the customization menu: the
   preview must remain visible while that menu is open.

6. Open Settings > Add-ons and find CurvedHUD. Depending on console library routing,
   it may be supplied by LibHarvensAddonSettings/LibVotans rather than the LAM panel.

7. Test live Health loss, Stamina/Magicka use, a damage shield, Major Resolve, and
   Balance/Equilibrium/Spell Symmetry. Bars should crop from the top without stretching.
   A shield above maximum Health should produce a pulsing gold edge on the Health arc.

8. If anything fails, capture every [CurvedHUD] chat line or Lua error verbatim.
   /curvedhud status reports the exact Health values returned by ESO.

9. Confirm the three main fills have a dark-to-bright ESO-style gradient. Toggle
   "Show default ESO resource bars" off and on. Off should hide only ESO's stock
   player resource frame and lower the self-buff row by about 40 pixels; On should
   restore both without changing CurvedHUD's scale or placement.

10. Cast Bound Aegis, Major Resolve, and Balance. Aegis and Resolve should have
    matching horizontal radii as vertical mirrors, while the Aegis icon remains
    in its previous position. Resolve's icon should sit lower/left, Balance's icon
    lower/right, and the orange Balance curve must remain visible over Health.

11. Apply and let a damage shield expire repeatedly. Each shield must begin from
    zero current shield, show only its live amount, and disappear completely on
    removal. New shields must never inherit a half-bar phantom value.

12. Mount and spend Mount Stamina. Its green auxiliary curve should appear only
    while mounted (or in Preview): outside in Stacked mode and hugging the selected
    side of Stamina in Parallel mode. Test both Stamina Inside/Top choices.

13. In Parallel mode confirm the outer Magicka/Stamina resource has a visibly
    larger concentric radius than the inner resource. Mount Stamina should hug
    the chosen Stamina radius, and Balance should hug Health's inner edge.

14. Hide the default ESO resource bars. The Buff/debuff vertical offset slider
    should become active and move ESO's self-buff row vertically. It should be
    disabled and ignored while the default resource bars are shown.

15. Balance should now form a clearly curved, close inner neighbor to Health;
    its icon and timer should not have moved. In Parallel mode Mount Stamina
    should be thin and sit five pixels inside Stamina rather than covering it.
    In Stacked mode it should sit outside the shared resource arc. Inspect all
    fixed arc tips for softly rounded rather than square cut ends.

16. Balance's upper-left point should remain fixed while the curve extends farther
    right. Parallel Mount Stamina should move farther inside when Stamina is inner,
    but outside when Stamina is outer. Stacked Mount Stamina should be slightly
    closer than 0.4.15. With stock resources hidden, verify the buff/debuff offset
    slider spans -750 to +750 and moves only when the setting or layout is applied.

17. Cast Balance and verify its orange tracker is only a smooth lower-curve
    segment, with no full bow, reversal, or vertical tail. Enable out-of-combat opacity, choose a low value, and
    verify the whole custom HUD fades out of combat and returns to full opacity
    immediately upon entering combat. Preview mode should remain full opacity.

18. Change Lower-left major buff from Major Resolve to another timed Major buff.
    The lower-left outside tracker should follow the selected buff's duration and
    icon. Select None and confirm it remains hidden outside Preview Mode.

19. Switch Inside timer thickness and Outside timer thickness independently.
    Balance should follow the Inside choice. The selected Major buff and Bound
    Aegis should follow the Outside choice. Confirm timer fill remains accurate.

20. Change each timer color preset and verify only its corresponding tracker
    changes. Test both Parallel and Stacked resource layouts at HUD scales 1 and 1.2.

21. Adjust Resource value font size and Resource percent font size. Health,
    Magicka, and Stamina labels should resize together without changing their
    established anchors or overlapping at their default sizes.

22. Under Global Trackers, toggle Balance off and on. Test its eight position
    choices and several colors in Preview Mode.

23. Under Sorcerer Trackers, toggle Bound Aegis off and on. Test its eight
    position choices and several colors in Preview Mode.

24. Compare upper/lower inside and outside Thick positions. Inside curves should
    not kick outward at the base; outside curves should follow a broad arc rather
    than looking pinched near the middle.

25. Change Major buff, Balance, and Bound Aegis choices on one character, switch
    characters, and confirm those choices are independent. HUD scale, spacing,
    sizing, opacity, and general layout should remain shared.

26. Confirm Inside and Outside timer thickness appear once in the shared layout
    controls and affect every skill in their respective position type.

27. Confirm Global and Sorcerer titles do not open submenus and every setting
    immediately below them remains selectable. Inspect all timer ends for clean
    rounded caps without oversized circles.

28. Change Balance and Bound Aegis through all eight positions using the console
    settings menu. Navigation must not skip unexpectedly, the HUD must not error,
    and each tracker must immediately move to the selected slot.

29. Inspect outside upper Thick, outside upper Thin, and outside lower Thin.
    Upper Thick should sit slightly closer; upper Thin should retain its midpoint
    alignment while fitting at the tip; lower Thin should sweep farther from its
    upper-left endpoint to its lower-right endpoint.

30. Compare every upper slot to its lower counterpart and every right slot to
    its left counterpart. Their curves must be exact vertical/horizontal mirrors.
    Inside Thin slots must use the established lower-left Balance geometry.

31. On the right, verify Thin trackers sit near the inner Mount Stamina radius
    and outside Thick trackers no longer float far beyond the resource curves.
    Confirm raw values, maximum values, and percentages appear above tracker toggles.

32. Confirm outer Thick and inner Thin remain unchanged. Inner Thick should sit
    farther inward. Outer Thin should sit inward with a slightly smaller radius,
    bringing its lower/far end closer than in 0.6.4.

33. In Parallel, verify both lower-right slots are closer to their resource bars.
    In Stacked, test all four right slots: upper inside/outside must remain on the
    upper resource half and lower inside/outside on the lower resource half.

34. Specifically compare Top Right - Inside and Bottom Right - Inside in Stacked.
    Their displayed halves must match their names rather than appearing exchanged.

35. Inner Thick must move horizontally toward screen center on both left and right.
    Outer Thin must retain its midpoint placement while its far endpoint bends
    closer to the parent resource arc.

36. Compare inner Thick with 0.6.6: it should sit three pixels less inward and
    use a tighter curve. Outer Thin should retain its established center-side
    contact while its far endpoint moves substantially closer to the resource arc.

37. Put a tracker in Upper Right - Outside and another in Lower Right - Inside.
    Their icons must be shifted away from the timer endpoints: farther outward
    for upper outside and farther inward for lower inside.

38. Confirm outer Thin and inner Thick retain their good midpoint anchors but
    use visibly broader, less tightly bent radii than 0.6.7.

39. Check icons in every quadrant. All inside icons must sit toward screen center;
    all outside icons must sit away from screen center, with left/right positions
    forming horizontal mirrors and no icon covering a timer endpoint.

40. Check both upper-inner icons: they should sit closer to their tracker/stat
    bars than in 0.6.8. Check both upper-outer icons: they should sit substantially
    farther from screen center and must not cover the tracker or stat bar.
