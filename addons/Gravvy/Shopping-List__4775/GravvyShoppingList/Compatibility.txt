Shopping List compatibility baseline
====================================

Verified for the 0.18.0 hardening cycle on 2026-08-25:

- ESO APIs 101050 and 101051
- LibAddonMenu-2.0 2.0 r43 / AddOnVersion 43
- LibMainMenu-2.0 4.5.0 / AddOnVersion 40500
- LibSets 0.9.3 / AddOnVersion 0009030

Manifest minimums reflect the oldest supported library interfaces. LibSets
0.9.0 remains the functional minimum because Shopping List uses API calls that
are present there; 0.9.3 is the tested and recommended release.

AwesomeGuildStore and LibCustomMenu remain optional integrations.

WritWorthy 7.5.8 is verified as an optional crafting integration. When both
WritWorthy and LibCustomMenu are enabled, Master Writ context menus can send
the writ's calculated material requirements to the current shopping list.
Shopping List calls WritWorthy.ToMatKnowList and does not read its saved
variables or alter its queued-writ state.
