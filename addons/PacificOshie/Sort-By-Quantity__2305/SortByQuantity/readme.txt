SortByQuantity provides a way to sort your inventory by item quantity.

This addon displays a clickable header to sort items by quantity in
your backpack or craft bag, your bank, house storage, and the guild bank.
Also provides sorting materials to refine at crafting stations,
and sorting materials for crafting at the alchemy and enchanting stations.
This means the listing will be ordered by how many there are of each item.

SortByQuantity by @PacificOshie.  Have fun!
