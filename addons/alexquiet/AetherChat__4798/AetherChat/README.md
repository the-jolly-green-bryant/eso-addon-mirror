A modern, immersive Skyrim & Tamriel-inspired Chat & Messenger replacement for **The Elder Scrolls Online (ESO)**.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📦 **Real-Time Loot Feed & 1-Click Need Requests (Synced with LootLog):**
  - Instant Right-Click Context Menu on any drop:
    - Whisper the looter directly with the pre-filled item link.
    - Ask in group channel (`/p`) with 1 click.
    - Link drop in active text entry.
  - High-visibility item icons, plain-text traits `(Divines, Impenetrable...)`, and golden `!!!` markers for noteworthy set pieces.
- 🛡️ **Collapsible Guild Folders (`[-] Guilds (5)`):**
  - Expand/collapse your 5 guild discussions in 1 click.
  - Switch guild chats directly without typing `/g1`..`/g5`.
- 📋 **Drag & Drop Channel Reordering:**
  - Hold left-click on any channel/contact tab to reorder your sidebar with an illuminated gold glow border.
- 🔔 **Dedicated Whispers & High-Audibility Alerts:**
  - Most recent private conversations jump to the top of the sidebar.
  - Red unread notification badges and dedicated `X` close buttons.
  - 7 crystal-clear audible alert ringtones (Champion Point chime, Triumph fanfare, Modern Ding, Magic Bell...).
- 👥 **Online Friends Quick Menu:**
  - Instant dropdown filtering **only online friends** with character names and current zones.
  - 1-click whisper without manual command typing.
- 📐 **Direct Edge Resizing with Luminous Glow Affordance:**
  - Hover over the right, top, or bottom border to illuminate the edge in radiant gold glow.
  - Smoothly drag to adjust window width and height (560x320 up to 1920x1200) with automatic dimension memory across sessions.
- ✉️ **Mailbox & Live Unread Badge:**
  - Direct inbox access with real-time unread mail counter.
- 🎨 **5 Immersive Tamriel Themes & Full Bilingual Engine:**
  - Themes: *Skyrim Dragonborn* (Cyan Blue), *Dwemer Gold* (Antique Brass), *Nordic Emerald* (Forest Green), *Crimson Brotherhood* (Dark Red), *Dark Glass* (Modern Indigo).
  - 100% Bilingual in **French** and **English** with immediate `/reloadui` button.

## ⚡ Optimal Setup & Recommendations

For the best experience, AetherChat completely replaces the native ESO chat box:

1. **Bind a Keyboard Shortcut:**
   - Go to `Esc ➔ Controls ➔ AetherChat Section ➔ Show / Hide`.
   - Toggle your messenger open and closed effortlessly at any time.
2. **Hide Official ESO Chat:**
   - Go to `Esc ➔ Settings ➔ Addons ➔ AetherChat` and check **"Hide official ESO chat"**.

## 💻 Manual Installation

1. Download `AetherChat_v2.30.0_Release.zip`.
2. Extract the `AetherChat` folder into your ESO AddOns directory:
   - `Documents\Elder Scrolls Online\live\AddOns\`
3. Launch ESO and enable **AetherChat** in the Add-ons menu.
