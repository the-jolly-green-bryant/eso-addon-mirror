---------------------------------------------
   The Elder Spores Online - Addon (v2.2.1) 
---------------------------------------------
is a complementary assistant to make your every-
day life just a little bit easier. 

It allows you to easily export your build creations, from
inside the game, directly to TheElderSporesOnline.com's 
Build Editor.  From there, once you have an account created
(by logging in with Discord), you will be able to edit, 
save, and even share your builds with ease.**

It also allows you to export shared public builds you like  
from TheElderSporesOnline.com, into the game for easy in-game
tracking and recreation.

* Created by Reishi_Spores for All to Enjoy.
* Subscribe to support this project !!!


---------------------------------------------
  WHERE/HOW TO PROPERLY INSTALL THIS ADDON:
---------------------------------------------

Place the Folder "TheElderSporesOnline" which has 3 files inside of it, 
directly in your "...Documents\Elder Scrolls Online\live\AddOns\" folder.

Log into ESO, make sure the Addon is enabled , and Enjoy.
If you are already In game , Type /reloadui , and Enjoy.
