##

Instructions for use

The addon is based around slash commands in chat, for example,

"/nso"     - will open a 'list of commands' for the Normal Sanctum Ophidia trial
"/nso 1"   - will put the first part of the guide into chat

The addon has the following options

The ability to have the instructions displayed in various chat channels including as a private message only you can see.
The ability to toggle the 'list of commands' in chat 'on' & 'off'. 
The ability to have a pop up window appear containing the 'list of commands', the pop up window can be moved, resized via the settings menu and will remember its position and dimensions. It also auto resizes based on the trial selected.
Both the settings menu & pop up window have slash commands (/ttg & /ttgui) or you can define a keyboard shortcut for them.
Damage mitigation settings for the red cp tree are included for the veteran trials.
