## Title: NewCrown
## Description: Adds a icon above the group leader's head. This add-on also lets you configure when it needs to be shown and when it needs to be hidden. A set of icons are included aswell. When the same functionality is enabled on other addons it could overwrite this addon.

## Version: 1.0.3
## APIVersion: 101038
## SavedVariables: NCSavedVars
