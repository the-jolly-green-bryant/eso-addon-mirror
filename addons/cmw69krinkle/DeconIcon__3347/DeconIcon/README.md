This addon is a personal project. I was sick of hovering over every piece of
equipment that I wanted to deconstruct to see if it was in the bank or not. I
prioritize deconstructing or researching the items in the bank over in my
character's inventory.

In order to alleviate my suffering, I wrote this addon to place a icon directly
on the inventory list to show where the item was when at the deconstruction screen.

There are two options provided. One to put an icon for items in the bank, the other
to put an icon for items in the current character's backpack (inventory).

Note:

This add-on probably doesn't play well with other addons. Please report any errors
you find. I will try to address them.
