ALDREN'S TIMERS — PS5 TEST 06

Purpose:
Confirm that the timer panel can automatically hide while pause-style menus are open.

Changes from 0.0.5:
- Added a saved on/off setting named Hide While Menus Are Open.
- The setting defaults to ON.
- The panel hides when ESO enters menu UI mode and returns when gameplay resumes.
- Timer detection, position, sizing, and display logic were not changed.

PS5 test:
1. Confirm the active timer still appears during normal gameplay.
2. Open Inventory and then the Craft Bag. Confirm the timer box disappears.
3. Close the menus. Confirm the timer box returns.
4. Open Settings > Add-On Settings > Aldren's Timers.
5. Turn Hide While Menus Are Open OFF. Confirm the timer remains visible in menus.
6. Turn it back ON and confirm hiding resumes.
