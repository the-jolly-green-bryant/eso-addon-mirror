## Title: Completionist's Lists
## Author: @IceQieen
## Description: Export Lists in CSV format of your characters known set items, Motifs, Provisioning Recipes, and Furniture Recipes. Export list of earned achievements. Export list of all antiquities. Export list of missing weapons and jewelery from trials. Also possible to export a list of all motifs and recipies in the game. Much thanks to the creators of MasterRecipeList, CraftStore, and DisplayLeads!
