--- Port to Jo -- Elder Scrolls Online - Addon -- Readme

To use "Port To Jo" ESO Addon to port to your Group Leader or Group Partner, there are two (2) options:

1) (Always available) in the Chat Window, simply type:  /ptj (group Leader) or /ptp (group partner)

and/or

2) Use a Keybind (a key you assign to trigger this addon)
Initially, NO key is assigned; you must assign a key by entering game settings,
  (usually by pressing ESC), then select CONTROLS / Addon Keybindings / Port to Jo.
Assign a Key: 
  (be careful to use a key not already assigned to another game or addon function, or a key you are OK REassigning)
Once assigned, pressing this key will trigger the teleport.

NOTE: Keybinds are per CHARACTER, so you may need to repeat the assignment for each one.


Remember the usual conditions for teleporting to your group leader, like:
- You must be in a group.
- You are NOT the group leader.
- The group Leader is in a location you CAN port to.
- You are NOT in combat.
- .... etc.

The Port-To-Partner only works if you're in a 2-person group, and will port you to the other player
