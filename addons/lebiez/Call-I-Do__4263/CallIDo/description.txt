Add-on for groups without voice chat to allow for organization and timing in PvP (can be used anywhere, but there are probably better options for PvE).

Press one to nine commands, configured as desired, to display a message for your group: "push", "leave combat", "heal", "focus him", everything is adjustable.

Library required for this add-on:

LibAddonMenu-2.0 LibGroupBroadcast LibGroupSocket

Please enable "Enable Sending" so that the notification is sent to your group.
