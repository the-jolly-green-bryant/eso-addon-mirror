[SIZE=5][B]Quick Menu[/B][/SIZE]

[IMG]https://i.imgur.com/CFsqrM9.jpeg[/IMG]

Ultivite includes a compact Quick Menu for the settings I am most likely to change while actually playing.

Press [B]Enter[/B] to open ESO chat and the Quick Menu appears. It closes when chat closes, or you can use the [B]X[/B] in the top right.

You can also open it directly from [B]Settings > Addons > Ultivite > Quick Access[/B].

The Quick Menu provides direct controls for combat information, navigation, warnings, world UI, graphics profiles and core HUD options. Champion Points are always shown on Champion player group frames and player target frames; Ultivite does not provide an option to disable them.

If you do not want Ultivite controlling target frames, use [B]RESTORE VANILLA TARGET FRAMES[/B] in the Quick Menu. This is a one click return to ESO's normal stock target frame.

[LIST]
[*][B]Preview[/B] shows supported Ultivite HUD elements directly where they actually appear on screen
[*][B]Resize[/B] is separate from movement and enables mouse wheel resizing for supported elements
[*]Weapon / Spell Damage is always directly draggable while visible
[*]Front Resistance is always directly draggable while visible
[*]Back Resistance is always directly draggable while visible
[*]Damage Shield strength is always directly draggable while visible
[*]The PvP K/D counter is always directly draggable while visible
[*]The CC immunity / immovability icon can be unlocked and dragged directly
[*]Fancy Action Bar can be moved and resized through Preview and Resize using its supported controls
[/LIST]

The Quick Menu is grouped into the same sections described below.

[SIZE=5][B]Ultivite[/B][/SIZE]

I originally made Ultivite for myself, based around the sets I use and the information I actually wanted visible while playing ESO.

It ended up growing into a much more feature filled addon, so I decided to share it.

I built and tested it on a [B]4K monitor[/B], so I am still interested in feedback from people using lower resolutions.

[SIZE=5][B]Combat Information[/B][/SIZE]

Ultivite is designed to show useful combat information without covering the screen in large permanent windows.

[SIZE=4][B]Simple PvP K/D Tracker[/B][/SIZE]

A compact kills and deaths counter for supported PvP areas.

It can be toggled from the Quick Menu and dragged directly whenever it is visible.

[IMG]https://i.imgur.com/iDIEVH4.jpeg[/IMG]

[SIZE=4][B]Live Damage, Resistance and Shield Stats[/B][/SIZE]

Optional clean text displays for:

[LIST]
[*]Weapon / Spell Damage
[*]Front bar Resistance
[*]Back bar Resistance
[*]Current Damage Shield strength
[/LIST]

There is no large background window around these stats. They are intended to be small values you can place exactly where you want them.

[SIZE=4][B]Target Frames, Champion Points and Vanilla Mode[/B][/SIZE]

Ultivite makes it deliberately easy to stop using its target frame.

Use [B]RESTORE VANILLA TARGET FRAMES[/B] in the Quick Menu, or [B]Vanilla / Default Target Frames[/B] under [B]Profiles & Core HUD[/B]. This disables Ultivite's custom target frame, disables its native overhead target replacement and releases target frame suppression so ESO owns the normal target frame again.

The enemy health bar control also includes a [B]Vanilla[/B] mode.

Champion Point display is intentionally not optional:

[LIST]
[*]Champion player targets always show their CP in Ultivite target frames
[*]Champion group members always show ESO's native CP number and Champion icon in stock group and raid frames
[*]Non Champion players keep their normal level display
[*]There is no Ultivite setting that turns group-frame or target-frame CP off
[/LIST]

When you choose Vanilla / Default Target Frames, ESO's own stock target frame presentation is left under base game control.

[SIZE=5][B]Graphics Profiles[/B][/SIZE]

The Quick Menu includes two clear one-click graphics actions:

[LIST]
[*][B]Apply PvE Graphics[/B]
[*][B]Apply PvP Graphics[/B]
[/LIST]

There is no separate profile selector. Press the profile you want and Ultivite applies it immediately.

Only the seven settings listed below are changed.

[B]PvE Graphics Profile[/B]

[LIST]
[*]Shadows: Ultra
[*]Screen Space Water Reflections: Ultra
[*]Distortion: On
[*]Bloom: On
[*]Grass / Clutter: Ultra
[*]Ambient Occlusion: Screen Space GI
[*]Sunlight Rays: On
[/LIST]

[B]PvP Graphics Profile[/B]

The same seven settings are switched Off, including Ambient Occlusion and Sunlight Rays.

No other ESO graphics settings are changed by these profiles.

[SIZE=5][B]Sound Recorder and Sound Blocker[/B][/SIZE]

Ultivite includes a sound identification and blocking system.

You can:

[LIST]
[*]Start sound capture
[*]Make the unwanted sound occur in game
[*]Identify the ESO sound being played
[*]Add it to the persistent suppression list
[/LIST]

I originally added this because there are several set, ability and interface sounds in ESO that I personally do not like hearing repeatedly.

[SIZE=5][B]Account Wide or Character Settings[/B][/SIZE]

Ultivite supports account wide or per character settings.

Normal addon updates preserve existing SavedVariables, HUD positions, scales, visibility choices, trackers and sound suppression settings.
