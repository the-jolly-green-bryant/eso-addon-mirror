GoldMailHelper

What it does:
- Saves a fixed recipient account and gold amount
- Fills the current send-mail window with recipient, amount, subject, and body

How to use:
1. Open a mailbox and go to Send Mail
2. Open Settings > Addons > Gold Mail Helper
3. Set the target account and amount
4. Press Fill Current Mail or use /goldmail
5. Review and send manually

Notes:
- This is designed to help mail gold quickly and safely
- It does not bypass mailbox rules or force hidden sending
