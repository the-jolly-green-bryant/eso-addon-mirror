## Title: VampStage
## Description: Displays your vampire stage with a skull icon and stage timer. Shows "Mortal" when you are not a vampire. Each element (skull, stage label, timer) can be moved, scaled, and toggled independently. Use /vamplock to unlock and drag elements, or /vampdebug to check buff detection.
