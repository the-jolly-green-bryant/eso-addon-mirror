[B][SIZE="5"]ESO ADVENTURER SUITE[/SIZE][/B]
Author: HoZayyBadazz
Version: 0.29.475
Status: Public Beta

ESO Adventurer Suite is an all-in-one Elder Scrolls Online utility suite focused on replacing scattered single-purpose tools with one coordinated, performance-conscious interface. Major systems cover combat guidance, action bars, builds/loadouts, inventory/crafting, minimap/exploration, questing/travel, character gear, antiquities, group tools, HUD overlays, diagnostics, and quality-of-life features.

[B][SIZE="4"]FEATURE HIGHLIGHTS[/SIZE][/B]

[B]COMBAT & ACTION BARS[/B]
* Smart best-next-action recommendations for DPS, Healer, Tank, and hybrid setups
* Two-bar action-bar presentation with active-bar emphasis, timers, stacks/procs, Skill Styles, bindings, Ultimate information, and unusable-skill fading
* Boss mechanics assistance, BLOCK NOW warnings, combat statistics/reports, and Infinite Archive Verse/Vision recommendations
* Integrated Health/Magicka/Stamina recovery-tick tracking inside the Suite player frame

[B]BUILDS, GEAR & LOADOUTS[/B]
* MAX POWER build/respec guidance for skills, morphs, passives, Champion Points, and attributes
* Saved loadouts for gear, skills, Champion setup, attributes, food, and related preparation
* Gear, set, Champion, attribute, and companion optimization tools
* Custom character/companion gear presentation with adaptive layout, item-quality presentation, readable text backdrops, weapon organization, and appearance utilities

[B]INVENTORY & CRAFTING[/B]
* Suite inventory grid with rarity presentation, search/filtering, quickslot support, and native item interaction
* Native ESO context menus are preserved so ESO actions and compatible third-party LibCustomMenu entries can still appear
* Bank, Guild Trader, and crafting/deconstruction category integration
* Enchant+ for compatible glyph selection
* Potion/Poison Maker and Turbo Recipe & Style Learner

[B]MINIMAP, EXPLORATION & TRAVEL[/B]
* Suite minimap with fantasy-frame styles, alliance-themed Suite player arrows, quest/POI/service pins, and performance-focused live navigation
* ESO-native Skyshard data shown on the big map, minimap, and nearby 3D markers
* 3D resource, fishing, treasure, survey, lore, antiquity, and dungeon/trial chest assistance
* Wayshrine routing, Guild Homes, merchant/stable/guild travel tools, Treasure/Survey/Tribute clue locations, and antiquity assistance

[B]QUESTS, ACTIVITIES & CODEX[/B]
* Tamriel Codex workspace, notes/checkpoints, Quest Finder, active-quest tracking, Golden Pursuits, activity history, dungeon tools, Group Finder, Battlegrounds, Tribute, Zone Guide, and Home Tours

[B]HUD & QUALITY OF LIFE[/B]
* Player/Group/Target/Companion frames
* Quickslot, repair/recharge, FPS/latency, time, difficulty, stable, reticle, synergy, quest, Champion, and utility overlays
* HUD Layout support for Suite elements plus selected ESO-native request, notification, and compass-focused-information elements

[B]DIAGNOSTICS[/B]
* /easscan - quick Suite/addon diagnostic
* /easbugs - recent captured errors
* /easbugs last - latest full error/stack
* /eastestall - safe Suite self-test
* /eastestall copy - opens a copy-friendly full diagnostic report
* /eastestall last - shows the most recent self-test report
The self-test validates loaded modules/libraries, secure Skills barriers, current-session errors, and context-sensitive Bank/Trader/Crafting/Skills checks without blindly executing protected gameplay actions.

[B][SIZE="4"]REQUIRED LIBRARIES[/SIZE][/B]
Install and enable these separately through Minion/ESOUI. The version numbers below are AddOnVersion minimums used by the manifest.

* LibAddonMenu-2.0 >= 43
* LibCustomMenu >= 730
* LibMainMenu-2.0 >= 450
* LibChatMessage >= 118
* LibDebugLogger >= 303
* LibMapPing >= 1240
* LibGPS >= 73
* LibMapData >= 122
* LibMapPins-1.0 >= 10047
* CustomCompassPins >= 138
* LibTreasure >= 24

The Suite does not hard-load embedded copies of these libraries. Keep standalone libraries current through ESOUI/Minion.

[B]INSTALL / UPDATE[/B]
1. Install/update every required library listed above.
2. When manually updating, remove the old ESOAdventurerSuite folder first so deleted or renamed files cannot remain behind.
3. Extract the ZIP to:
   Documents/Elder Scrolls Online/live/AddOns/ESOAdventurerSuite/
4. Confirm ESOAdventurerSuite.txt is directly inside that folder.
5. Enable ESO Adventurer Suite and all required libraries at the character-select Add-Ons screen.
6. Log in and open Settings > Addons > ESO Adventurer Suite.

[B]COMPATIBILITY NOTES[/B]
* ESO's native right-click item menu remains the authority for normal inventory items so other compatible addons can add actions through supported menu integration.
* Protected ESO Skills drag/pickup controls are intentionally left untouched.
* Context-dependent diagnostics report SKIP when the corresponding ESO scene is not open; SKIP does not mean the feature failed.
