## Title: JadeHelmetHide
## Author: @TheJadeSiren
## Version: 1.0.0 (2017.12.31)
## APIVersion: 100021
## SavedVariables: JadeHelmetHideSavedVariables
##
## Description: Adds a small UI icon and shortcut to quickly show/hide the user's helmet
##
## By default, the icon is created in the center of the screen and auto-hides unless the
## user is in "UI" mode (the '.' key by default). The icon can be moved around the screen 
## by dragging the edge of the icon to a new location. It can be toggled to always on so
## that the helmet status is always shown.
##
## "HIDE" implies that the helmet is visible. Click to hide.
## "SHOW" implies that the helmet is invisible. Click to show.
##
## You may also create a keyboard shortcut in the Controls/Keybindings menu in the game 
## under the Interaction section named JadeHelmetHide
##
## Shortcuts:
##   /hh or /helmet to toggle show/hide helmet
##   /jhh to toggle icon always on or auto-hide
