Individual Mode (/barras)
With the items unlocked, hover your mouse over the desired element and use:

Click and Drag: Moves the element anywhere on the screen.

Mouse Wheel (Scroll): Changes the width (for bars and trackers) or the overall size (for icons).

Shift + Mouse Wheel: Changes the height/thickness (for bars and trackers) or increases size faster (for icons).

Alt + Mouse Wheel: Changes the opacity (transparency) of the element.

Ctrl + Mouse Wheel: Changes the text scale (size of timer numbers and percentages).

Ctrl + Shift + Mouse Wheel: Rotates the icon (useful for setting skills in a diamond/rhombus layout).

_____

Combined Mode (/barrasall)
When the green frame is visible on the screen:

Click and Drag the Frame: Moves all icons and bars simultaneously, maintaining the distance between them.

Mouse Wheel (Scroll): Increases or decreases the scale of the entire set proportionally.
