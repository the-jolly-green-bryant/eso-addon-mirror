Better Buffs v0.0.06

- Target labels no longer show internal suffixes such as ^N.
- Processed hostile target death before the out-of-combat guard.
- Debuffs on a confirmed dead target now clear immediately, even when ESO reports combat ending first.
- Preserved natural post-combat expiration for living targets.
- Added no new tracker, cache, event registration, or update loop.

Better Buffs v0.0.04

- Removed the incorrect assumption that a hidden timer means an indefinite effect.
