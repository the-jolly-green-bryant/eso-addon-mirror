# Conductor v4.6.1-dev1.11

- Added an explicit Show Personal Assignments window setting.
- Personal Assignments now obeys its own visibility toggle in every render, idle, hide, and preview recovery path.
- Raid Setup saves now capture the live center position of every movable Conductor window before writing the profile.
- Buffs & Debuffs position restore now uses exact saved center offsets without silently reclamping or recentering the panel.
- Drag position saving now uses the control center point rather than reconstructing it from scaled dimensions.
