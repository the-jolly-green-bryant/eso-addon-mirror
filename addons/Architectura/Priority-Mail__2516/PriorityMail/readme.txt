Persistence is a virtue... and Priority Mail will persistently attempt to deliver your mail to any recipients that are less diligent about cleaning out their inbox.

How to use Priority Mail:
1. Send mail to any player who decides to let their inbox fill up frequently.
2. Carry on with your day.

Priority Mail will attempt to deliver your message with the persistence that your recipient should have had for maintaining a tidy inbox... and it will notify you via the chat window when your message is finally delivered. Hopefully sooner than later.

If you feel like checking up on Priority Mail (or if you wish to cancel a queued message), just open your Mailbox, click the Send Mail tab and click the "Priority Mail" postage stamp to see all of your queued messages.
