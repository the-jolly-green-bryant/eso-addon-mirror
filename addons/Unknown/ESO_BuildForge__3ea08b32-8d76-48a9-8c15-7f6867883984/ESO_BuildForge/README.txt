ESO BUILD FORGE v3.2 — APPLY GENERATED BUILD
================================================

THE TWO APPLY BUTTONS NOW DO DIFFERENT THINGS:

APPLY GENERATED
- Applies the build ESO Build Forge generated for your selected role.
- Searches your unlocked skills by the generated skill names.
- Places resolved skills on the active weapon bar.
- Equips owned armor/jewelry from the recommended sets in your backpack.
- Swap weapons once to apply the generated second skill bar.
- Reports missing or locked generated skills.

APPLY LOADOUT
- Restores the gear and bars you previously captured yourself.

HOW TO USE
----------
1. Open /esobuild.
2. Choose role and resource.
3. Press GENERATE.
4. Press APPLY GENERATED.
5. Swap weapons once to fill the other generated bar.

COMMAND
-------
/esoapplygenerated

LIMITS
------
- Generated skills must already be unlocked and morphed on the character.
- Recommended gear must already be in the backpack.
- Generated weapons are not automatically selected yet because main-hand,
  off-hand, and weapon type require an exact weapon plan.
- Apply outside combat.

INSTALL
-------
Replace the old ESO_BuildForge folder and type /reloadui.


VERSION 4.0 DAMAGE SIMULATOR
- Simulator tab with 4 target profiles
- Easy, Realistic, and Perfect execution models
- 30/60/120 second deterministic rotations
- Character-stat-aware power, resource, crit, penetration, and recovery
- Per-skill, light attack, and ultimate breakdown
- Sustain penalties and optimization warnings

Simulation values are estimates and should be validated with a target-dummy parse.
