## Description: Scans your equipped gear, inventory, bank, and ESO Plus bank for weapons/armor/jewelry and dumps a readable, copy-pasteable text report (item, slot, trait, quality, set name, and the exact set bonus text from the game) via /gearexport. Built for feeding straight into an AI for build advice.
