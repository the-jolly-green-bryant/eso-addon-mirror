## Deliberate exclusions

- Lair of Maarselok (zone 1123): pending final-arena identification. Maarselok appears in earlier encounters too, so zone plus name would warn where hardmode cannot be activated. No automatic reminder there yet.
- Icereach matches Mother Ciannait only; the sisters also occur before the final encounter.
- Fang Lair matches Thurvokun, not Orryn.
- Secret/optional bosses without their own hardmode activation, trials, and arenas are excluded.

## Matching and evidence

The addon uses zone ID plus formatted English boss name, not a permanent numeric NPC ID. Names in the same numbered group are aliases or members of one encounter. This list identifies encounters that offer activation; it does not inspect whether activation is currently available or already used. Earlier required hardmodes may still need completion in multi-hardmode dungeons.

Zone IDs: [LibZone data](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Baertram/LibZone/master/LibZone/LibZone_Data.lua). Encounter sources are linked per row. Additional activation references: [Imperial City Prison tome](https://elderscrollsonline.info/achievements/lord-wardens-retaliation), [Bedlam Veil guide](https://alcasthq.com/eso-bedlam-veil-dungeon-guide/).

Simulated Lua tests validate matching and suppression, not actual event timing, live boss name spelling, or activation objects. Please report the /hmdebug zone and boss name for missed reminders.
