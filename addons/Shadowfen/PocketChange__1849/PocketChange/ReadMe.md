PocketChange is a simple automatic manager for the currency that you carry in your pocket. You set levels for:
* gold, 
* alliance points, 
* telvar stones, and 
* writ vouchers 
and PocketChange will try to ensure that you always carry precisely those amounts.

It checks your currency levels whenever you talk to the banker and either deposits what you have that is over the limits you set, or it withdraws enough to bring you up to those levels (presuming you have enough in the bank to do so - it won't manufacture money for you, sadly).


Supply Notifications:

PocketChange will also check the numbers of basic supplies that you normally carry in your backpack and will notify you if the count is below the minimum that you set when you go to the bank. Supplies that it can check for are:
* soul gems
* repair kits
* lockpicks
* empty soul gems
* poisons equipped on the main weapon
* poisons equipped on the backup weapon

For supplies, it does not deposit to or withdraw from your bank. 

PocketChange just lets you know that you might want to fill up your supplies if they have gotten low. You can have it ignore the item, post a message in chat, or post a message in chat and make the alert sound.

When set to do so, it will list the item, the total number that you have in your pack, and the minimum number that you set in the options.
In the case of the poisons, it will list the total number that you have equipped instead of the number that you have in your pack - because you cannot use poisons from your pack until you first equip them.

Note: PocketChange does not look at the levels of repair kits or soul gems you have. It will count Commons with Grands and Crowns and so on to come up with a total number.
